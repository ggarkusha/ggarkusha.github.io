Meet the project ↗

Created by Professor Grigory Garkusha, this collection develops interactive models around research in algebraic geometry, topology, motivic homotopy theory, and algebraic K-theory.

Every exhibit identifies its constructions, hypotheses, and limitations. The models invite exploration while keeping the mathematical statements explicit.
